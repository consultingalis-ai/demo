    Uslovi kupovineTerms of sale

    Porudžbina je prihvaćena kada Vam pošaljemo potvrdu na mejl. Cene su konačne, sa PDV-om. Ako nekog komada nestane posle Vaše porudžbine, javljamo Vam se pre slanja i vraćamo novac za tu stavku.

    Boje na ekranu mogu malo da odstupaju od prave tkanine.

    An order is accepted once we send you a confirmation by email. Prices are final and include VAT. If a piece sells out after you order, we contact you before shipping and refund that item.

    DostavaDelivery

    • Kurirom AKS na adresu u Srbiji, rok 1–3 radna dana. Poštarinu računamo po težini pošiljke: zbir težina komada sa pakovanjem, plus 0,2 kg kutije.
    • Besplatna dostava za porudžbine od 7.000 RSD (iznos robe posle kupona).
    • Pouzeće: kurir naplaćuje naknadu 1,5 % od iznosa, najmanje 202 RSD, na ceo iznos koji plaćate kuriru (roba + poštarina). Naknada važi i kad je dostava besplatna.
    • Pošiljke teže od 50 kg: cenu dostave javljamo telefonom.
    • Lično preuzimanje u radnji, Kralja Petra 36, u radno vreme, kad Vam javimo da je porudžbina spremna. Bez poštarine i naknade.
    • AKS courier to any address in Serbia, 1–3 working days. Postage is based on parcel weight: the weight of the pieces with their packaging, plus 0.2 kg for the box.
    • Free delivery on orders from 7,000 RSD (goods total after the discount code).
    • Cash on delivery: the courier charges a fee of 1.5% of the amount, at least 202 RSD, on the full amount you pay the courier (goods + postage). The fee applies even when delivery is free.
    • Parcels over 50 kg: we will phone you with the delivery price.
    • Pick up at the shop, Kralja Petra 36, during opening hours, once we let you know the order is ready. No postage or fee.

    Pravo na odustanakRight of withdrawal

    Od kupovine možete da odustanete u roku od 14 dana od prijema robe, bez navođenja razloga. Pošaljite nam popunjen obrazac ispod ili izjavu mejlom. Novac vraćamo bez odlaganja, u skladu sa zakonom, istim načinom kojim ste platili. Troškove vraćanja robe snosi kupac, osim ako je drugačije navedeno.

    Roba treba da bude nenošena, sa etiketom. Izuzetak su komadi skraćeni ili prepravljeni po Vašoj meri.

    You may withdraw from a purchase within 14 days of receiving the goods, without giving a reason. Send us the form below or a statement by email. We refund you without delay, in line with the law, using the same payment method. The buyer pays the cost of returning the goods unless stated otherwise.

    Items should be unworn, with the label attached. Pieces shortened or altered to your measurements are excluded.
